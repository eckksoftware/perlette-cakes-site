# Brand Notes

Internal reference for brand, copy, and launch constraints.

## Core Positioning

- Motto: `Made With Love, Made With Care`
- Business: home-based baker
- Feel: warm, homemade, premium without feeling formal
- Priority: products should feel delicious first, decorative second

## Public Voice

- Use `we / our` for now
- Keep the tone personal, not corporate
- Keep factual business statements plain and consistent

## Visual Direction

- Product-first photography
- Editorial type, not generic bakery UI
- Warm cream base with rose-led accents
- `Fraunces` for display, `Zarathustra` for body and UI in the current implementation
- Keep the expressive wordmark treatment limited to the brand name
- Active logo asset: `public/perlette-logo.svg`

## Copy Constraints

- No public pricing until approved
- No fake testimonials
- No halal-certified claim unless verified
- No allergen-free claim unless verified
- Keep dietary discussions on WhatsApp

Use `CONTEXT.md` for canonical business, delivery, dietary, and order-process facts.

## Source Intent To Preserve

- Homemade, but premium
- Beautiful and delicious, with taste as a priority
- Suitable for real celebrations and gifting
- Strong ingredients, no corner-cutting
- Personal care from a home-based baker
